Is this medicine recalled?

A batch recalled in one country is often still on shelves in another. BatchWatch reads recall and counterfeit alerts from 10 regulators with NVIDIA Nemotron and checks a pack or a whole stock list in seconds.

  • Alerts from 10 official drug regulators
  • Every batch number checked against the regulator's own text
  • The pharmacist always makes the final decision

How BatchWatch knows

Regulators publish recalls as web pages, PDFs and tables, each in its own format. BatchWatch watches ten of them, reads every new alert with NVIDIA Nemotron, checks its own reading, and measures the result.

BatchWatch is a prototype built for the Nebius × NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon. Alerts are summarised from public regulator notices; read the original notice before acting. A pharmacist decides.
